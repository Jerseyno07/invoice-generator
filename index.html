One row per line item. Rows are grouped into one invoice per unique combination of ShipToAddress, BilledToAddress, GSTIN, PONUmber and Date — the same columns your export uses.
Required: BilledToAddress, skuName, Qty, Rate (Qty from BilledQuantity/SkuQuantity/FulfilledQuantity, Rate from invoiceSalePrice/sodSalePrice).
Optional: ShipToAddress, GSTIN, PONUmber, Date, InvoiceId, FSNCode, UOM, HSNCode, SubLineTotal, Tax, TaxAmount, FSSAI.
When InvoiceId is present, rows are grouped by it directly and that ID is printed on the invoice and encoded in its QR. The item table prints FSNCode (not SkuId), and when a row carries FSSAI it's shown on the seller side, overriding the field above for that invoice.
